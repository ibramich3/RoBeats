The ensemble

Built around your families, your songs, and your timeline

RoBeats is the percussion project of musician Romario Eskandar, based in Chicago and Northwest Indiana. The group specializes in Egyptian rhythms and traditional drums for the moments that define an Arab wedding: the grand entrance, the line dance, and the live set that keeps the reception in the pocket.

Every booking is custom. We match team size to the room, lock the set to your playlist, and play with your DJ or band so the night feels like one celebration — not two separate shows.

Meet the artist

Romario Eskandar

Percussionist and founder of RoBeats, based in Chicago and Northwest Indiana. Romario plays tabla and darbuka live, bringing Egyptian rhythm to the moments families remember most.

Questions

What couples usually ask

How long is a performance?

Entrances are typically short and powerful. Reception sets are planned around your schedule. We confirm length in your quote.

Where do you perform?

Chicago, Northwest Indiana, and surrounding suburbs. Travel fees may apply for farther venues.

Can we request specific songs?

Yes. Share your list and we will build the set around it — classic Egyptian repertoire and modern remixes.

What do you need from the venue?

Access to the entrance area, a short sound-check window, and a contact person on the day.

Do you play with a DJ or another band?

Yes. Live percussion sits on top of the existing program so the energy never drops between sets.
